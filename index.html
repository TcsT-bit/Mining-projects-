Mining SLP Projects – Contractor Code of Practice & Quality Commitment

1. Purpose

This code commits Twinz Cooperate Services (Pty) Ltd to delivering all mining Social Labour Plan and community projects to the required standard, on time, and with full transparency.

2. Quality & Technical Standards

  • Work executed to approved specification, BOQ, and SANS standards
  • No material substitution without written approval
  • All testing by SANAS-accredited labs. Failed tests rectified at our cost

3. Transparency & Documentation

  • Weekly progress reports, delivery notes, test results, site diaries submitted
  • Open project file for client and community oversight
  • Payment claims supported by evidence of work done

4. Anti-Corruption & Ethics

  • Zero tolerance for bribes and collusion. Report any attempt immediately
  • Pricing based on actual cost + fair margin. No underbidding to cut corners
  • Full compliance with MHSA, OHSA, CIDB regulations

5. Project Delivery & Accountability

  • 14-day notice + recovery plan if cash flow issues arise
  • Accept standard retention and 12-month defects liability
  • All compliance docs open for verification

6. Tender Guarantee & Failure Response

Our Commitment: If we fail to deliver, we will:

  • Notify client 14 days in advance with written recovery plan
  • Allow client to call on performance guarantee/retention to complete work
  • Hand over all completed work and records for continuity
  • Cover cost of rectifying defective work in defects liability period

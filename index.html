Supplier Vetting Checklist

  • CIPC registration active and verified
  • Tax clearance / SARS good standing certificate valid
  • B-BBEE certificate or sworn affidavit
  • Products have valid SABS/SANS certificates
  • Manufacturer warranty minimum 12 months
  • Product liability insurance minimum R2,000,000
  • 30-day trading terms or bank letter
  • Written confirmation of stock and lead time
  • 2 recent client references verified
  • Tax invoice, delivery note, SABS certs provided for audit

Supplier Agreement - Key Clause 7

7.1 Standards & Certification: Goods shall conform to BOQ spec and comply with SANS/SABS standards. Valid certificates provided on delivery.

7.2 Inspection & Rejection: Goods failing spec may be rejected within 7 days. Supplier replaces at own cost within 14 days.

7.3 Warranty: Minimum 12-month defects liability. Defects rectified at no cost within 14 days.

7.4 Liability & Indemnity: Supplier indemnifies Twinz against claims from defective goods. Product liability insurance required.

7.5 Payment Hold: Twinz may withhold payment for defective goods until rectified. Recovery costs recoverable from Supplier.

Why this matters: Mines get burned by fake steel and failed school furniture. We vet suppliers, check certs, take delivery photos, and give you the full audit pack. If it fails, we chase the supplier.

Mining SLP Projects – Contractor Code of Practice & Quality Commitment

1. Purpose

This code commits [Your Company Name] to delivering all mining Social Labour Plan and community projects to the required standard, on time, and with full transparency. The goal is to restore public trust in how mining funds are spent.

2. Quality & Technical Standards

We commit to executing all works strictly to the approved project specification, BOQ, and relevant SANS/SANS-based standards.

  • No substitution of materials, equipment, or methods without written approval from the Engineer/Client
  • All material testing, inspections, and certifications will be conducted by SANAS-accredited laboratories and qualified professionals
  • Any work that fails testing or inspection will be rectified at our cost and within agreed timelines

3. Transparency, Reporting & Documentation

  • Weekly progress reports, delivery notes, test results, and site diaries will be submitted to the client
  • A project file will be maintained and made available for client and community oversight on request
  • All payment claims will be supported by evidence of actual work completed and verified

4. Anti-Corruption, Ethics & Compliance

  • We do not offer, give, or accept bribes, gifts, or inducements to secure contracts or conceal poor workmanship
  • We will immediately report any attempt at collusion, intimidation, or interference with QA/QC processes to the client’s ethics/compliance line
  • Pricing submitted is based on actual cost + fair margin. We do not underbid to win and compromise quality later
  • We comply with all relevant South African laws including the Mine Health and Safety Act, OHSA, and CIDB regulations

5. Project Delivery & Accountability

  • We commit to completing the project as contracted. If cash flow or supply issues arise, we notify the client 14 days in advance with a recovery plan
  • We accept standard retention amounts and commit to the full defects liability period as per contract
  • Our CIDB grading, tax clearance, COIDA, and project references are open for verification at any time

6. Local Empowerment & Community Benefit

  • Where required, we will prioritize local labor, SMMEs, and suppliers in line with SLP and local procurement targets
  • We will participate in community engagement sessions during and after project handover
  • Health, safety, and environmental standards will be upheld to protect workers and the community
